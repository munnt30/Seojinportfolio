About Me

I grew up on Jeju Island, surrounded by volcanic landscapes and ocean stories. I started animating flipbooks at 12, and now I work in Procreate, Toon Boom, and Figma.

I'm interested in how animation and interaction design both ask the same question: how does this make someone feel?
